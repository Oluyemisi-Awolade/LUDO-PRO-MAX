Privacy Policy — Ludo Pro Max

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ludo Pro Max ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information (via Firebase Authentication):

  • Email address
  • Display name

Gameplay Data:

  • Win/loss record
  • ELO rating
  • In-game coin balance
  • Games played

Online Multiplayer Data:

  • Room codes you create or join
  • In-game chat messages sent during online matches
  • Your chosen player color/token position and turn data, shared with other players in the same room in real time

Analytics: The App does not currently use any analytics or advertising SDKs. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety disclosure on Google Play.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your player account
  • To save and display your gameplay statistics (wins, losses, ELO, coins)
  • To enable real-time online multiplayer matches, including syncing game state and chat between players in the same room
  • To display leaderboard and tournament standings

3. Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google): Used for authentication and real-time database storage of account and gameplay data. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Flutterwave: The App includes an optional "Support the Developer" donation link. Tapping it opens Flutterwave's own website in your browser, outside the App. We do not collect, receive, or store any payment or financial information you may enter there — that is handled entirely by Flutterwave under its own privacy policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication, both operated by Google. We apply access-control rules to this database to restrict who can read or write data. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and gameplay data are retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Pro Max is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
